Audio description

A local HLS fixture (fixtures/audio-description/DemoVideo) with a main audio rendition (a 440 Hz tone) and a second rendition marked CHARACTERISTICS="public.accessibility.describes-video" (the tone at 20% with 880 Hz "descriptions" at 1–3 s and 5–7 s). The manifest lists it under audio_descriptions, and the audio menu lists "English (audio description)" alongside the original track; the descriptive WebVTT is added as a hidden <track kind="descriptions"> and stays out of the captions menu.
